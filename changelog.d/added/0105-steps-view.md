- **Steps View: one level at a time.** A new view (**Ctrl+S**) draws the node you stand on as a header card and its direct children as cards beneath it, washed in their aspect's colour. A card shows the fields you would open the editor for, its first Info notes, and how many children it holds ("3 of 12"). The arrows move between cards, **Enter** steps into one (moving the same location the breadcrumb and **Ctrl+O** use), **Shift+Esc** / **Ctrl+Esc** climb out, **E** edits and **Space** cycles a status. Cards are created and deleted with the Mindmap's keys, and the **+** beside the header lists the kinds the Step can hold. A wide Step turns to pages: **PageUp** / **PageDown** or **[** / **]**, and the arrows carry over a page edge; card size is set per tab. A folded run of Habit history is a card you can step into, level by level.

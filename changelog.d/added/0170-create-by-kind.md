- **Create a node of a given kind with Shift and its initial.** On the Mindmap and in the Steps View, **Shift+D**, **Shift+P**, **Shift+G**, **Shift+T**, **Shift+C**, **Shift+I**, **Shift+E**, **Shift+F** and **Shift+H** create a Domain, Project, Goal, Task, Commitment, Info, Expectation, Flow or Habit under the selection. Most drop you straight into naming it; Commitment, Flow and Habit open their editor first (**Shift+H** opens it with Repeating already on). Where the kind cannot live, nothing is created and a notice says where it can. Inside a Flow template the same keys create flow items: Task, Goal, Commitment and wait items.

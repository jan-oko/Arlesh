- **Undo and redo.** **Ctrl+Z** reverses the last thing you did to the board, and **Ctrl+Shift+Z** or **Ctrl+Y** redoes it, in every view. One gesture is one step: a multi-node paste, a cascading delete, a drag that clamped descendants, or saving an editor (a save refused partway is rolled back in full). A notice names each step ("Undid: paste 5 nodes"), and an empty stack says "Nothing to undo". Inside a text field Ctrl+Z is the field's own undo. Undo does not cover filters, presets or navigation, never reverses changes made by an agent or a script, and starts empty each session.

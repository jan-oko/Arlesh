- **The top bar now says which subtree you are in, in both views.** Going into a part of the board used to show you only the ways *out* of it — an `↑` pill back to the whole board and a `←` pill up one level — and nothing naming where you had actually landed. The centre of the bar now carries a small subtree glyph and the name of the place you are in, so `CODE` reads as `CODE` rather than having to be inferred from the level above it. It sits unadorned — no pill, no border — because it is a label rather than a control: the two pills on the left are the ways out, and "here" has nowhere to go. This shows up in the **Mindmap** as well as the List View, which had exactly the same gap.

- **Expectations: waits your tasks depend on.** A new kind of node for something outside your own action that you are waiting on — a training run finishing, someone replying. Create one with `Shift+W` anywhere a Task can live; it holds notes, is Pending until you release it (`L`, `Enter` or a click on its triangle), and can be archived. A task that depends on a pending Expectation reads as blocked until it is released, and the Task editor's dependency picker offers Expectations beside Tasks and Goals.
  Give one an optional **check-by** — the scope picker opens in Duration form — and a "check on it" task appears beneath it for that window; completing it (`D`, or its status control) clears the check-by and leaves the wait pending. The List View has an **Expectations** option (`Alt+E`) showing every pending wait, and Plan and Start show them by their own rules.
  Two opt-in settings tie waits to asynchronous work: marking a task Asynchronous can ask what it is waiting on, and finishing an asynchronous task that waits on nothing yet can offer to record it. A delegated task now waits on a derived Expectation of its own, released when the task is done.

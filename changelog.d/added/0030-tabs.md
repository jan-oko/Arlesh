- **Tabs.** Several places on the board can be open at once, each tab remembering its whole view: the subtree you entered, the view, the branch direction, every filter, the selection, collapsed branches and the canvas position. **Ctrl+T** opens a tab where you are (with fresh filters), **Ctrl+W** closes one, **Ctrl+Tab** / **Ctrl+Shift+Tab** cycle and **Ctrl+1**–**Ctrl+9** jump by position; these keep working while a dialog is open. The strip above the top bar has a **+**, an **×** per tab, closes a tab on middle-click and reorders by dragging. A tab is named after where it is (**All** for the whole board); right-click → **Rename tab** gives it a name of its own, and emptying the box hands it back to the automatic name. Closing a window's last tab closes the window.

  The clipboard, the theme and the display settings are shared between tabs; **Vertical layout** is per tab. Tabs come back when you reopen Arlesh — order, names, location, view and filters, but not the selection, collapsed branches or zoom. Your existing view reopens as the first tab.

  **The board alone:** **F11**, or **F** with nothing selected, hides the tab strip and the top bar; press it again to bring them back. Arlesh always reopens with them shown.

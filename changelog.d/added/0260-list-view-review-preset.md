- **Review option in the List View.** The preset dropdown (and the phone's preset sheet) ends with Review, also on `Alt+R`: it lists every Task that is waiting on your answer or review, even one that is blocked, set aside in the Backlog or planned for later. A sub-item shows only if it is in Review itself. Like Unblock and Expectations it applies to the List View only, and the Mindmap keeps its preset. `Ctrl+F` filter search now lists Under and Depends on after every other group.

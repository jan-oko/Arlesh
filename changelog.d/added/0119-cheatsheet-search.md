- **A tabbed keyboard cheat-sheet you can search.** The cheat-sheet (`Ctrl+Shift+/`) now shows one tab per area — Global, Filters, Tabs, each view and the Scope pickers — and opens on the view you are in, marked *here*. A tab's shortcuts sit in two columns, and the long ones (Global, Mindmap, List View, Steps View) are split under headings such as Create and Edit. Switch tabs with a click or with `←` / `→`.
  A search field is focused when the sheet opens: type part of a description (`flow`) or a key (`shift+t`) and the matching shortcuts from every tab are listed under their headings, with a message when nothing matches. `Esc` clears the search and returns to the tab you were on; a second `Esc` closes the sheet.

- **Stage answers, then send them together.** An agent's question now has **Stage** and **Send** beside its answer field. Stage writes your answer down without sending it (press it again to unstage and edit); `Ctrl+Enter` stages, and `Ctrl+Enter` again in the staged field sends. **Send** sends every staged answer on the board at once, so an agent that asked three questions is woken once, with all three answered, instead of acting on the first while you write the rest. A review works the same way: Approve, Approve and merge and Request changes now only choose your verdict, and Stage and Send follow. Staged answers are saved on the server and show on your other devices, and the agent sees nothing of them until you send. A send button with the number staged appears in the top bar while anything is staged (even with the agent status turned off) and in the phone's command palette; a Task is marked staged once all its open questions are. If an answer's question was answered or removed meanwhile, the rest are sent and a message says which was skipped. One `Ctrl+Z` takes a whole sent batch back and stages the answers again.

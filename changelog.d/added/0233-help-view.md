- **Help, with the derivation graph.** A new Help view shows how the app's states are worked out: every stored input, derived state and preset as a box, with arrows from what each is computed from. Hover or focus a box to see what it is and what it reads; click, tap or press Enter to pin its card, and Clear to let go. Open it with F1, from Settings on the desktop, or from the ☰ menu on the phone; the × button or Esc returns to the view you were on, exactly as you left it.

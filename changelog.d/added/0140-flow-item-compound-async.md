- **Compound and the wait template on flow Task items.** The flow item editor now has the Task editor's **Compound** switch, and under its **Asynchronous** switch the same wait-template fields — title, tags, Time Scope and Check every. Every Task the item spawns takes them: starting a Flow copies them onto the Task it makes, and each Habit occurrence reads them from its item.
  An occurrence can say otherwise in its own editor, or with `V`: switch Compound on or off for that occurrence alone, or give it a wait of its own — or none, by emptying the section. A compound occurrence's status follows its sub-items as a compound Task's does, and is blocked when all of its open sub-items are; its iteration no longer waits on it, since its steps are what is done.

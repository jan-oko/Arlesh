- **A Part-of-Day cycle can be planned.** A flow item's Cycle Scope of a part of the day had no finer kind to plan within, so its occurrences could never count as planned. Its picker now has a **Plan to scope** switch: on, the cycle is planned into its own part of the day — a Noon cycle into Noon — its row reads "Noon · planned", and every occurrence it draws is planned there in the Plan View and the filters. Off, the default, leaves it unplanned as before.

- **Plan one habit occurrence on its own.** Double-click a habit's task occurrence to give that one occurrence its own Plan — a day, a part of the day or a range, with the same Plan field a Task uses — or to leave it deliberately unplanned this time. Next time round it follows the habit's usual Cycle Plan again, and "Follow the Cycle Plan" hands this one back as well. The occurrence's calendar badge shows the plan it actually has, drawn in the accent colour when it is the occurrence's own, and struck through when you took the plan away. A plan outside the occurrence's own window is refused, a future occurrence can be planned ahead, and editing the habit's scope or repetition now asks before discarding occurrences you planned this way. Undo reverses it like any other edit.

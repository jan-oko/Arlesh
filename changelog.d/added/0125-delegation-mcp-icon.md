- **Delegation you can see, and agents can set.** A delegated Task now wears a badge in its status row in every view that draws one (Mindmap, List, Steps, Plan and Zen): a paper plane, whoever holds it. Hovering says "Delegated to the agent" or "Delegated to <name>". The wait a delegated Task carries now reads as who is to finish it, for example "Agent finish: Write the release notes" or "Tuli finish: Book the venue". A wait you gave a title of your own keeps that title. Agents can delegate through the MCP: `arlesh_tasks.create` and `update` take `delegate`, set to `{"kind": "agent"}`, `{"kind": "person", "id": N}`, or `null` on update to take the delegation back.

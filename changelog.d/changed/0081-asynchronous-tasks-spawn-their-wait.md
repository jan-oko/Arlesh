- **Finishing an asynchronous task starts its wait.** An asynchronous task now says what it will be waiting on: a title, tags, a Time Scope and how often to check on it. Completing the task creates that wait beneath it, as an Expectation you release, archive and check on like any other; reopening the task takes the wait back. Tasks that were already asynchronous start with a wait titled "Waiting on …" after the task.
  Edit it from the Task editor, where turning Asynchronous on shows its fields, or with `Shift+W` on a task; `W` still turns it on or off in one press. A task that depends on an asynchronous task now stays blocked after that task is done, until the wait it started is released.

- **A List View row's colour now says what state the work is in, not how deep it sits.** A row is
  tinted by its status — blocked, archived, frozen, done, in progress, or open — with a Commitment
  read on its Verdict. The old rule shaded a node's aspect colour by its depth, which said the one
  thing a flat list never needs to say, now that the path header names where a row came from in
  words.

- **A card's colour now says what state the work is in, not how deep it sits.** In the List View and
  the new Steps View alike, a row or card is tinted by its status — blocked, archived, frozen, done,
  in progress, or open — with a Commitment read on its Verdict. The old rule shaded a node's aspect
  colour by its depth, which said the one thing a flat list and a single Step never need to say, and
  left some cards unreadable: an Aspect's own card was drawn in its full-strength colour with dark
  text over it. The aspect colour is still there, as a bar on the leading edge.

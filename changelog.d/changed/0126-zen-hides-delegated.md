- **Zen shows only what you hold.** A delegated Task no longer gets a card on the Zen grid, even while it is In Progress. Do still lists it. A card you have just delegated stays where it is until you move the selection off it. In Zen's Filter menu, a new **Agent finish waits** switch (`d` while the menu is open) hides the waits on Tasks delegated to the agent from the Expectations strip. Waits on Tasks a person holds stay. While those waits are hidden, the Filter button shows its dot.

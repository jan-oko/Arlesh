- **Writes show at once and other devices follow within a second, without reloading the board.** After a write the app no longer fetches the whole board again: the server (or, in Standalone, the app itself) works out which nodes changed and sends only those, and every window and device applies them to the board it holds. A change over a Client-mode link therefore arrives in a few hundred bytes instead of 1.6 MB, and a phone that dropped off the network or went to the background is caught up with what it missed rather than reloading. Time alone now moves the board too: a Task that goes Overdue, or a lock that lifts, appears within a minute with no write to trigger it. The app also checks, every minute and when it is idle, that its board is the one the server published, and loads it afresh (and logs a bug) if it is not.

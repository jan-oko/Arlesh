- **A Steps card is coloured by the aspect it lives under**, so a Step reads at a glance as one
  place on the board rather than a set of unrelated cards. It is not the aspect colour painted on:
  a small share of the hue is mixed into the card background, which keeps every aspect legible in
  both themes — including a pale one, and including an Aspect's own card, which used to be drawn in
  full-strength colour with dark text over it. What state a card is in is left to its icon and its
  badges, which already carry it.

- **Android landscape goes edge to edge, following the phone's real shape, and the touch layout no longer depends on width.** The bars, the Mindmap canvas, the lists and the Zen cards now run to the screen's edges, under the camera cutout and the side navigation bar, in both landscape orientations. Only what is really in the way is kept clear of: the bars' ends stay inside the screen's rounded corners, and a bar, row or card that the camera overlaps pads its content clear of it. In Full screen the Zen cards fill nearly the whole screen, up under the status bar, with their outer corners following the screen's curve. The phone's top bar, bottom navigation and action bar now show whenever touch is the only pointer, so a phone in landscape no longer shows the desktop bar, the tab strip and the action bar all at once; the tab strip shows only without it.

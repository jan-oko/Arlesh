- **Android landscape goes edge to edge, and the touch layout no longer depends on width.** The bars' backgrounds and the Mindmap canvas now run under the camera cutout and the side navigation bar instead of stopping short and leaving a strip, in both landscape orientations; buttons and labels still keep clear of them. The phone's top bar, bottom navigation and action bar now show whenever touch is the only pointer, so a phone in landscape no longer shows the desktop bar, the tab strip and the action bar all at once; the tab strip shows only without it.

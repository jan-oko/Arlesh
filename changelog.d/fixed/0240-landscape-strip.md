- **Android landscape goes edge to edge, and the touch layout no longer depends on width.** The bars, their buttons, the Mindmap canvas and the Zen cards now run under the camera cutout and the side navigation bar instead of stopping short and leaving a strip, in both landscape orientations; the List, Plan and Steps views still keep their rows clear of them. The phone's top bar, bottom navigation and action bar now show whenever touch is the only pointer, so a phone in landscape no longer shows the desktop bar, the tab strip and the action bar all at once; the tab strip shows only without it.

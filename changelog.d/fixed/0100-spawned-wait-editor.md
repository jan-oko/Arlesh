- **`E` on the wait an asynchronous task spawned opens that wait's own editor.** It used to open the task's editor, so the task's Plan looked as though it belonged to the wait. The Expectation editor now opens on the wait: its status and archive are yours to change there, and its title, Time Scope and Check every show what the task's Expectation template says (change them in the task's editor, `Shift+W`). The wait has no Plan and takes none from its task: in the Plan View, a check task under the wait is no longer refused because of the task's Plan. A delegated task's wait still opens the task's editor, since nothing on that wait can be edited.

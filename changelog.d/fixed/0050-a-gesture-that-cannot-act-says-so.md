- **A gesture that cannot act says so, instead of doing nothing.** **Delete**, **Shift+Enter** and **Ctrl+Shift+Enter** on an Aspect now explain that Aspects are fixed, and a selection holding an Aspect refuses the whole delete rather than deleting the rest. Every refusal from the backend — on paste, rename, status changes and creation — is now shown with its reason, and a drag no longer offers a target it cannot write to.
